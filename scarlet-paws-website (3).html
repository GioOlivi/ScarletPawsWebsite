Our Mission

Compassion in action on every campus.

Scarlet Paws Animal Welfare Network is a non-profit organization dedicated to the humane treatment of stray and feral cats found on the New Brunswick campuses of Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey.

We serve cats that have been abandoned, become lost, or born on campus through our daily feeding programs, TNR efforts, and adoption placement services.

We are a collaboration of Rutgers staff, faculty, students, and community volunteers. Co-founded in 2011 by Mary Ann Cancio, who continues to serve as President.

TNR Program

Trap-Neuter-Return

TNR is the humane and effective method for managing feral cat populations on campus. We trap, spay/neuter, vaccinate, and return cats to their outdoor home — stopping population growth humanely.

T

Trap

Humane traps set by trained volunteers across campus feeding areas to safely catch feral and stray cats.

N

Neuter

Cats receive spay/neuter surgery, rabies vaccination, health check, and an ear tip to identify TNR cats.

R

Return

Cats return to their outdoor home where volunteers continue daily feedings and monitoring.
